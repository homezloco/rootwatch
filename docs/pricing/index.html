How billing works

Upgrade in the dashboard

Paid plans are purchased inside the app — sign up free at app.rootwatch.dev, then open Org Settings → Plan and check out through Stripe. RootWatch never sees your card number.

What limits are enforced

Plans gate three things: org seats (member invitations), active API tokens (rw_…), and hosted AI analysis. Everything else — the dashboard, REST API, MCP server, CLI, and desktop app — works on every plan.

BYOK AI

On the Free plan you can still use AI features by configuring your own provider key (OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek) under AI Settings — you pay your provider directly. Pro and above unlock the deployment-hosted provider.

Managed service

Prefer it run for you? Canadian Web Consulting operates the control plane, onboards your machines, and sends monthly posture reports — billed as a per-client retainer, not per-seat.

Self-hosting

The agent, CLI, and desktop app are Apache-2.0 and point at any RootWatch instance. The control plane is closed-source; the hosted service at app.rootwatch.dev is metered on seats, tokens, and hosted AI — not on the client code.
