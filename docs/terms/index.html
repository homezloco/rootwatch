Terms of Service

The rules for using the hosted RootWatch control plane at app.rootwatch.dev.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. The service

RootWatch is a host security control panel operated by Canadian Web Consulting ("we", "the operator"). It provides a web dashboard, REST API, MCP server, CLI, desktop app, and fleet agent that collect and analyze security telemetry from machines you enroll. By creating an account at app.rootwatch.dev you accept these terms.

2. Accounts

Registration creates your user account and an organization. You are responsible for your credentials and for the API tokens (rw_…) you mint — treat them like passwords and revoke any that leak. You must be authorized to accept these terms on behalf of the organization you register.

3. Authorized use only — this matters

RootWatch monitors and can act on real machines. You may enroll, scan, inventory, and remediate only hosts you own or are explicitly authorized to administer. Using the service to probe, scan, or act on third-party systems without authorization is prohibited and may result in termination of your account.

  • Remediation actions are approval-gated; you approve each privileged change.
  • Guarded actions (e.g. stopping a listener) re-verify targets before executing, but the decision to run them is yours.

4. Plans and billing

Plans and limits are described on the pricing page: a free tier, paid self-serve plans billed monthly in USD through Stripe, and a managed service invoiced separately by Canadian Web Consulting. Paid subscriptions renew until cancelled and are managed in the dashboard under Org Settings → Plan. Plan limits (seats, active API tokens, hosted AI) are enforced by the service; exceeding a limit returns an upgrade prompt, not silent failure.

5. Acceptable use

  • No unlawful activity, and no monitoring of systems you are not authorized for.
  • No attempting to access other organizations' data; all data is org-scoped.
  • No abusive load, probing of the service itself, or interference with other tenants.
  • Findings, reports, and scores are informational — they are evidence collected from your hosts, not a guarantee of security.

6. Your data

Telemetry reported by your enrolled hosts belongs to your organization. We process it to provide the service as described in the Privacy Policy. Credential values never leave your machines — only fingerprints and redacted paths are reported.

7. Self-hosted deployments

If you point the agent, CLI, or desktop app at a control plane operated by someone else (including yourself), your relationship for that telemetry is with that operator, and these terms govern only your use of the hosted service.

8. Availability

We aim to keep the hosted control plane available but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. The status page describes how service health is reported. A security monitoring tool is not a substitute for your own backups, alerting, and operational judgment.
