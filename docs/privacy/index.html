Privacy Policy

RootWatch is a security product — it works by collecting telemetry from machines you enroll. This page describes exactly what that is.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Who we are

RootWatch is operated by Canadian Web Consulting ("we"). This policy covers the hosted control plane at app.rootwatch.dev and the rootwatch.dev website. If you self-host RootWatch, your own deployment's operator is responsible for the data it collects — this policy does not apply to third-party instances.

What we collect

  • Account data — the username/email and password you register with. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (scrypt); we cannot read them.
  • Host security telemetry — when you enroll a machine via the agent, CLI, or desktop app, it reports the data the product is built on: listening-port inventories and the processes that own them, host metrics, security-check results and findings, vulnerability data, network discovery results, and device posture. This telemetry is scoped to your organization.
  • Credential inventory metadata — the credential scanner reports a sha256 fingerprint, the last four characters, and a redacted path for each key it finds. Secret values never leave your machine.
  • API tokens — rw_… tokens are shown once at creation and stored only as hashes.
  • Session cookie — the dashboard uses one session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Billing

Paid plans are processed by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe's checkout directly — RootWatch never sees or stores card numbers. Stripe's own privacy policy governs the data they process.

AI features

When you use hosted AI analysis (paid plans) or configure your own provider key (BYOK — OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek), the prompts and context needed for the analysis are sent to that AI provider under their terms. AI provider keys you configure are stored by the service so it can make requests on your behalf.

How we use the data

  • To operate the service: dashboards, findings, reports, alerts, and the API.
  • To enforce plan limits (seats, active API tokens, hosted AI access).
  • To secure the service: audit logging, abuse prevention, incident response.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your host telemetry to train models or for advertising.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run RootWatch — Stripe for billing, our hosting provider for infrastructure, and the AI provider you select for AI features — or where legally required. We do not share org telemetry with other customers: all host data is org-scoped.

Retention

Account and org data is kept while your account is active. Security-event retention is configurable per deployment (the default is 365 days). If you want your account and associated data removed, contact us via canweb.net.

Your responsibilities

Only enroll machines you own or are authorized to administer. The telemetry described above is collected from enrolled hosts — enrolling a machine you are not authorized to monitor is a misuse of the service (see the Terms of Service).

Self-hosting

RootWatch's agent, CLI, and desktop app can point at a control plane you operate. In that arrangement your telemetry stays on infrastructure you control, and this policy applies only to the extent you interact with the hosted service.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions or data requests: reach us through Canadian Web Consulting — see the contact page.
